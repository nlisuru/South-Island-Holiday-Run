    Starter itinerary

    Seven days,
    southbound.

    A one-way taster from Christchurch to Te Anau. Treat it as a starting point, not a schedule to race through.

    If you have more time, add a night rather than another long drive.

    See a longer official itinerary ↗
    1. Day 01

      Christchurch

      Arrive, rest and explore the city. If you have flown a long way, avoid starting a big drive straight away.

    2. Day 02

      Christchurch → Aoraki / Mount Cook

      Travel through the Mackenzie Country, pausing at Takapō / Lake Tekapo and Lake Pūkaki on the way to Aoraki.

    3. Day 03

      Aoraki → Wānaka

      Check DOC conditions for a morning walk, then continue over Lindis Pass to the laid-back lakeside town of Wānaka.

    4. Day 04

      Wānaka

      Keep a full day for the lake, a viewpoint, a cycle trail or simply a slower morning with a mountain backdrop.

    5. Day 05

      Wānaka → Queenstown

      Make your way to Queenstown and spend the afternoon beside Lake Whakatipu or exploring the town on foot.

    6. Day 06

      Queenstown → Te Anau

      Choose a morning activity, then travel to Te Anau, a useful base for exploring Fiordland National Park.

    7. Day 07

      Milford Sound / Piopiotahi

      Leave plenty of time for the road into Milford Sound. Check weather and road conditions before setting out from Te Anau.

    Know before you go

    Good trips start with good prep.

    Open each note for a practical starting point, then use the official links to check current conditions.

    Driving: allow more time

    New Zealand drives on the left. Roads can be narrow, winding and slower than a map suggests, so rest properly and take regular breaks.

    NZTA visitor driving guide ↗
    Weather: pack for change

    Bring layers and check the forecast before alpine walks or long drives. For tracks, also check DOC alerts and only go within your experience and equipment.

    MetService forecasts ↗ DOC safety advice ↗
    Bookings: plan the popular parts

    Great Walk huts and campsites can be in high demand. Check the official booking information early and keep an alternative in mind.

    DOC booking information ↗
    Care: leave only footprints

    Stay on marked tracks, take rubbish with you, keep a respectful distance from wildlife and follow local signs and closures.

    Department of Conservation ↗
